# BottomlessRack

*A seamlessly integrated gear spawner with unlock-based progression*

Buy eligible gear, bring it back to your PMC, or claim it from mail to unlock it permanently. Each unlock automatically examines the item for your PMC and Scav, including standard weapon parts earned with a receiver. Automatic examination grants no XP or Intellect points. You can make free copies even after you lose the original.

Use EFT’s equipment picker, modding, builds and presets views to seamlessly create free copies of unlocked equipment.

| In EFT | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Inspect window | An unlocked item’s description starts with **UNLOCKED**, including when you inspect loot in a raid. |
| Equipment slot | Select a row tagged **Unlocked** to create and equip a compatible item. Weapons use their standard build. |
| Modding | Fit an owned part or choose **Unlocked** to create a copy. On weapons, armor, and other gear, swapping parts sends a loaded magazine to the stash with its rounds. Other replaced parts, including empty magazines, are discarded with their contents. Remove a part first to keep it. Hover over a blocked part to see the conflict. |
| Edit Build | Assemble a weapon preset using owned parts and free copies of missing unlocked parts. Assemble stows removed magazines that still contain ammo. Other unused parts it strips off are discarded with their contents after a successful assembly. Remove a part yourself before assembling if you want to keep it. An unlocked weapon body can also be supplied. EFT offers to buy any remaining locked parts. |
| Weapon context menu | Right-click an owned weapon and choose **Spawn spare magazines** to add empty copies of its fitted magazine to your main stash. A 1×1 magazine gives **3** spares, a 1×2 or 1×3 gives **2**, and every other shape gives **1**. A notification confirms how many arrived. |
| Equipment presets | Equip reuses matching builds, down to the attachments, plates, and packed contents. Other builds stay intact. Missing unlocked gear is supplied automatically, and EFT offers to buy the remaining shortages. |
| Trader Buy tab (except Fence) | Pay the usual price or barter cost for gear you haven’t unlocked. Once you can make a free copy, the offer shows **Unlocked** and the trader disables the deal. You can still use unlocked gear as payment for other items. |
| PMC trader sales and flea listings | Traders, including Fence, decline gear in the mod’s categories. You also can’t list it on the flea. Take covered gear out of a container before selling the container. Ammo, medicine and barter goods follow the usual sale rules. |
| Post-raid Scav Sell All | Sell your remaining Scav loadout to Fence at the normal price. Gear you transfer to your PMC follows the PMC sale rules. |

The PMC sale restriction applies to looted, purchased and spawned copies, even before a template is unlocked. It blocks Ragman’s **Circulate** quest, which requires selling backpacks or tactical rigs. Offers already on the flea can finish normally.

Weapons use their published default build when one is available and usable. Otherwise the mod chooses the complete trader offer with the fewest fitted parts, then the lowest loyalty level. A weapon with no complete offer can be issued bare if it already has every vital part. Earning a weapon also earns its standard parts, including on existing profiles.

In Edit Build, **Unlocked** marks parts that Assemble can supply for free. Choo² Trader Modding’s **Quick Buy Items** makes a paid trader purchase. With WeaponBuilderSearch, newly earned parts appear the next time the slot list opens after a catalogue refresh.

Modding and Edit Build are available from the stash before you construct the Workbench, including the Edit Build button on the bottom menu.

The catalogue picks up gear in your PMC’s equipment and stash, including Scav loot you transfer, collected crafts, and purchases from Fence or the flea. A customized weapon unlocks its standard factory parts alongside the parts you recovered.

Each spawn request needs room in the main stash for the whole batch.

## Profile changes

Permanently unlocked gear is stored in your profile.

After uninstalling the mod, SPT can still load and save the profile with the unlocked catalogue present, and reinstalling the mod restores access to its unlocks.
